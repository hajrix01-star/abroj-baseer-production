# PURCHASE-DAILY-REPORT — build contract and live record

- Baseline: main `fee40097e441626b66290fe9ab192194ab0d3b04`; independent branch `codex/purchase-daily-report-20261122`; impact ARCHITECTURAL (financial data and authorization).
- G0 draft: owner, accountant, branch manager need a custom Baseer interface, daily expandable totals, filters for invoice date and current payment status, supplier bill details and return preserving context. Direct and batch-generated invoices are represented once via posted `account.move`; no accounting writes. Explicitly label credit notes and partial/in-progress states; draft/cancelled bills excluded. Original invoice opening is not required: bounded read-only detail is necessary for branch managers without general accounting access.
- G1 draft: existing Odoo deployment, assume up to 10 simultaneous readers and 10,000 bills/year/company, retention and recovery follow existing accounting; assumptions unmeasured, no capacity claim. Server-enforced date range and pagination, including daily detail, prevent unbounded browser reads. Avoid new load environment: delta is a bounded read-only report, usage unknown and no specific new risk justifies a load test.
- G2 draft: financial authority Odoo posted vendor invoices/credits and current residual/payment state; aggregate by company currency with signed totals, distinguish source currency and show exchange-related limits where needed; never sum currencies blindly. Company and role validated server-side for every call; narrow fields only, no general invoice/accounting access for manager, no client-supplied privilege, independent access and cross-company tests. Use existing Baseer roles `group_owner`, `group_accountant`, `group_cashier` (branch manager), exclude `group_pos_cashier`. Date is invoice date, payment state current not historical; no changes to ledger, reconciliation or operational records.
- G3 draft: existing Odoo 19 OWL/assets and Python/ORM; custom designed local screen, no Odoo table design or new libraries; reuse existing services/translations where applicable. Adapt Baseer workspace entry and its allowlist to each role without bypassing company checks. Action read-only; endpoint must validate even when called outside UI.
- G4 plan: original responsive cards/expandable rows with keyboard and screen-reader semantics; Arabic/English RTL/LTR; Western digits and server-provided formatted values; no nonessential motion. Review 390px and desktop.
- Verification plan: direct and batch-backed invoice counted once, refunds, payment states incl. partial/in payment, company and forbidden IDs, user groups, different currencies, date boundaries, pagination and navigation; visual desktop/mobile and isolated Odoo tests if runtime available.
- Gatekeeper: independent review required before implementation; G0–G3 currently DRAFT. Direct route: one read-only report over posted bills avoids duplicate batch aggregation, while custom detail avoids broad accounting grants.

G2 currency and completeness amendment after independent financial review: label totals as payments allocated to these invoices; company-currency amount uses reconciliation allocation, which may differ from actual bank cash under FX and must be disclosed in report and document details. A confirmed payment lacking a journal entry/allocation, or pending payment, must produce an explicit incomplete/pending notice rather than an unqualified zero; direct bank-statement allocations require posted state, company and outbound/inbound direction appropriate to bills/refunds. Refuse to represent write-offs, credit notes and exchange differences as payments. Owner-facing invoice-currency numbers are amounts allocated to the invoice, not proof of bank-date cash flow. Confirmed user request is met without inventing an FX allocation or silently omitting unsupported settlement.

Pending payments are separately flagged and excluded from confirmed paid values. Same-company-currency, confirmed posted payment/statement allocations may be summed, with refunds negative. Invoice-currency allocation may be shown as such, explicitly qualified; no inferred exchange allocation. Other limits and tests in 8–9 remain. Include tests for this case and a payment cleanly allocated across multiple bills. Pending is separately identified; no-move payment is unavailable.
