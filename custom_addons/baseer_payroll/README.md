# Baseer Payroll and Employee Advances

This Odoo addon extends the installed payroll stack with payroll operations,
employee advances and loans, settlements, end-of-service workflows, related
corrections, work schedules and printable payroll documents. It depends on
`om_hr_payroll_account`, `hr_holidays` and `baseer_report_layout`.

Payroll calculations, accounting entries, access rights and employee records
remain in Odoo's server-side models and the underlying payroll modules. This
addon does not provide a separate ledger or bypass native accounting and HR
permissions.

Deployment and recovery follow `docs/build-governance/BUILD-GOVERNANCE.md`.
