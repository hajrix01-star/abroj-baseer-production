## R1 — G0–G4 design decision

Read `BASEER-PAYROLL-PROFILE.md` and the current version/employee salary implementation. **G0–G4 GO for implementation within the specified QA scope, with the binding amendments below.** The user explicitly chose an independent EOS calculator with an approved native disbursement bill, native Odoo schedule hours, one salary profile and live split preview. No additional user permission is needed for this design. A native supplier bill and native payment registration provide the existing accounting authority; a new payment ledger or automatic employee termination is not justified.

1. **One version and one hours source.** Use selected `version_id` consistently for displayed/editable salary and its `resource_calendar_id.hours_per_day`, without changing the current contract when a historical version is selected. Native calendar hours may be fractional (8.5/10.5); do not truncate them through an Integer field. Feed Decimal-converted hours into the shared split helper and keep the separately explained monthly calculation basis. Dynamic unsaved preview must use the same helper as persisted/approval calculation, with no browser arithmetic. Invalid or empty intermediate form values may show a validation message/empty preview, but invalid allowances or split inputs must not be saved for payroll-enabled employees. Existing posted slips stay immutable; a change in schedule must not silently rewrite posted values.
2. **Salary rights and original fields.** Merge the view into the native Payroll page while preserving native HR/contract/calendar rights and the existing Baseer payroll-manager restrictions. Hiding a duplicate field is not a permission boundary. Keep all intended native contract fields available to their original authorized roles. Record any change in draft salary calculation caused by the new schedule source; do not bulk rewrite user schedules, contracts or salary inputs to make tests pass.
3. **EOS policy and evidence.** Reusing SA-EOS-V1 from the old system is an explicit calculation policy, not proof of universally correct legal entitlement. Before final acceptance, verify applicable formula/reason thresholds/wage basis against an authoritative Saudi source and record any remaining policy limitation. Service date inclusion, fractional years (including leap dates), resignation thresholds and exclusion of overtime must be explicit and tested at exact boundaries. Special exemption/legal reasons require user-provided evidence and a verified confirmation, not automatic inference. Estimate creation/change has no accounting or termination effect. Approval must verify the selected employment period/version and actual departure evidence match the request, without changing employee departure information automatically.
4. **One award and durable approval identity.** Lock request then consistently ordered employee key before final source revalidation and posting. Block both same-request concurrent approval and a second approved request for the same employee/service-ending event, while allowing a genuinely different employment period after rehire. Keep an irreversible approved/issued identity: deleting or canceling the bill must not cause an approval replay to issue a replacement. A nullable bill relation alone is insufficient. Test effective create defaults, forged financial links/state, child/source changes and request reparenting. The source snapshot/policy/evidence becomes immutable once approved.
5. **Native bill integrity and correction boundary.** Validate company, native accounting permission, purchase journal, expense/payable setup, partner, currency and lock dates. Explicitly clear invoice-line taxes and any product/default tax source. Create exactly one balanced posted in_invoice only on explicit approval, all-or-nothing. Ensure the source-linked employee/company, monetary award/currency, tax-free line and financial linkage cannot be edited afterward into a different obligation; use native reversal for allowed correction with its origin trace. Cancellation/deletion must follow native access/accounting rules and retain the durable no-reissue condition. Show native canceled/reversed/partial/paid states faithfully. Loan recovery and unrelated final settlement remain outside this calculator and cannot be silently netted from the award.
6. **Focused acceptance and delivery.** Cover selected historical version, unsaved preview, fraction-hour schedules, allowance boundaries, native role separation, EOS reason/date/threshold/zero/basis edge cases, estimate with no accounting, explicit posted bill, native partial/full/reversal, double approval with independent transactions and duplicate service event, company isolation and Financial Record navigation. Capture Arabic desktop/mobile and exact before/after financial preservation; main remains untouched. Reuse unchanged native components and prior finance tests where appropriate. The stated 500-employee/10-manager contract is a design target; the inherited observed capacity was only a 100-employee local measurement, and this delta must not claim a newly demonstrated SLA without evidence.

Keep native version ACL/company rules and all unrelated native contract/departure/calendar field groups unchanged. Do not use sudo or imply the HR-manager group. Record this deliberate salary-field permission extension in the final handoff. No actual employee user/group assignment is changed.
