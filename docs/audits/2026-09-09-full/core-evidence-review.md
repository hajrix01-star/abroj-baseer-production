## Gaps and decision implications

- **Bank settlement distinction:** `hr_services_extended_checks.json` reports `invoice_hook=paid`, `payment_states=[paid]`, `matched=[false]`, and `actual_in_payment_observed=false`. This is candid and valuable evidence of the installed native behavior. It must never be summarized as “bank reconciliation passed” or “in_payment journey tested.” The mocked alternative was correctly not used. No new defect is asserted merely from the native state; any product label promising confirmed bank receipt needs separate review.
- **Independent financial-statement reconciliation remains outside these five artifacts:** purchase−345 cash movement is a strong independent checkpoint, but the set does not establish where the net350 expense and tax52.50 appear in P&L/tax accounts/trial balance, nor exact paid-category totals. Parent/source trace consistency and move balance are insufficient substitutes. Root can use separate ledger/source-review evidence to cover this gap or retain it as an explicit limit.
- **Preservation strength differs:** shared-provider and HR-service tests compare selected table row hashes after rollback; seed tests compare IDs/settings and perform rollback without a full final hash; purchase tests primarily compare financial record counts. Do not turn these into a claim that all database fields or attachments are identical. QA/main preservation should use the separately captured root fingerprints.
- **Visual PDF acceptance:** the HR test checks `%PDF` and size; the strengthened purchase race stores a45,963-byte PDF. Root reports having visually inspected the Arabic purchase PDF; that independent rendered-page review is the relevant visual evidence, not byte count. This specialist did not inspect the pages. The earlier purchase regression runtime log contains missing original-clone filestore attachment reads; that does not invalidate its accounting assertions. HR PDF visual acceptance still needs its own evidence if claimed.
- **Fixture preparation is part of scope:** shared-provider tests assign company category properties, purchase tests create fresh category/service mappings and direct-payment journals, and HR payment tests switch payment method accounts to exercise supported paths. Successful fixture flows do not erase actual configuration blockers found elsewhere.

These are bounded evidence qualifications. The previously overbroad race-summary boolean is now supported and CORE-EVIDENCE-01 is closed. I found no additional mismatch requiring a new application finding. There is no reason to repeat the reviewed tests solely for this independent acceptance check. Root's ongoing upgrade rehearsal is separate and has not been accepted by this review before its results exist.
