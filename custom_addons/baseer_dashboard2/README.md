# Dashboard 2 accounting settings

Settings are per company and editable by accounting managers. Live identifiers below are only applied when the record exists and belongs to the expected company; skipped records are logged. Existing mappings are preserved. Review settings in each database. No cutover date is documented or seeded: when empty, the first qualifying real POS order in the company determines the cutover, independently of the selected period. Configure a date explicitly when the historical transition is known. Days before it use summary entries; days on or after it use real POS. A day containing both sources is flagged for review. Orders with absolute totals at or below the minimum (1.5 SAR by default) do not determine the cutover or count toward customers, ticket average or hours. Summary POS configurations can be selected in settings; names containing «ملخص المبيعات», «Sales summaries» or «ملخصات» are also detected. Their orders are not customers; their posted session moves provide summary sales.

All asset_cash accounts are liquidity automatically; additional accounts and their source kind/label are editable. Outflow is credit on liquidity accounts regardless of journal, excluding pure liquidity transfers and moves containing 999999. The older journal settings do not determine outflows. The documented POS payment method IDs remain seeded by company. The account codes 101007–101009 are never seeded as application channels. Unreceived applications are the lesser of mapped application account balances at period end and application gross sales; reset moves are excluded and a reset on the end date makes the value unavailable. Unpaid bills use posted bill payable balances less partial reconciliations through the period end.
