### Binding implementation details to prove before acceptance

1. **Payroll/loan accounting linkage:** Loan deductions may share one employee receivable accounting line across multiple loans, but the allocation must identify each loan/installment, applied amount and source line unambiguously. Reconciliation can only consume that employee/company's relevant debit entries. Allocation totals and accounting residuals must reconcile after every standalone repayment, payroll recovery and permitted reversal; external/manual reconciliation must not silently leave the loan register stale. Either reject unsupported manual alterations at the server boundary or derive/recheck balances from native ledger residuals before any further action.
2. **Partial repayment and deferral policy:** Freeze deterministic expectations before the six-month test runs. A repayment must reduce the remaining part of existing installments (oldest due first as documented), not add a second deduction or mark the full installment paid. A deferred unpaid amount retains its original date and history, acquires an explicit new due date, and never changes principal or recovered amounts. Explain whether overdue amounts are caught up in the next payroll. Approved history cannot be silently recomputed when the schedule changes.
3. **Concurrency/idempotence:** Apply the shared employee/company lock consistently to loan disbursement, repayment, payroll compute/approval and deferral, with sorted ordering in batch operations. State and residual checks occur again after locks. Link and reuse the existing posted operation on repeat approval/disbursement; reject stale/conflicting repayment or deduction amounts. A replay test alone is not proof of two independent simultaneous database requests.
4. **Posted source and native accounting controls:** Guard parent slips, salary children, loan/installment/allocation children and linked move/line mutation through public model methods and direct writes. Only narrow native reconciliation fields may remain writable; do not disable unrelated native accounting operations globally. Cancellation/refund must never call upstream destructive paths on a posted effect. If coupled reversal cannot be completed safely, block it with a translated explanation and retain the original audit trail as the contract allows; do not advertise delivered correction functionality.
5. **Company and role boundaries:** Global company rules must cover separately accessible child models, not just their parent. Validate employee/version/loan/journal/account/partner relationships server-side as applicable. A payroll manager in another company must fail reads/writes, including RPC-style operations. Protected operations require both payroll management and accounting rights, without sudo/context Boolean authorization shortcuts. Python-only identity token remains a private internal transport, never a user-supplied capability.
6. **Salary basis and input policy:** Clearly distinguish the configured gross salary used for the basic/included-overtime split from any separately added allowances and from final gross earnings. Preserve totals after rounding. Calendar-day versus fixed-30 proration is an explicit visible configuration, not an assertion of Saudi policy. Paid leave preserves agreed entitlement; only the explicit unpaid flag removes eligible pay. Unsupported partial-day unpaid leave and mid-period compensation changes must fail before posting, with no partial batch effects.
7. **Treasury and payment truth:** Native bank/cash payment and reconciliation determine salary paid/residual. A direct native journal entry used for advance disbursement or standalone repayment must be labeled as a recorded cash/bank transaction, not an executed external transfer. Avoid duplicated cash recognition when a bank statement is subsequently reconciled. Settling net salary across two methods must leave the payable residual and loan balance independently correct.
8. **Creation and atomicity:** Monthly generation must avoid duplicate employee-period slips and preserve manual draft inputs on refresh. Validation errors during an approval must not leave a partially posted batch unless the UI explicitly implements and reports a deliberate per-record workflow. Prefer the documented one-click atomic batch behavior. Empty draft handling must not silently erase populated entries.
