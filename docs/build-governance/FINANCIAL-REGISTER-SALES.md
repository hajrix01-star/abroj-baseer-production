# FL3 — native POS sales in Financial operations

Design candidate; independent G0–G3 approval is pending. Documentation only at this stage. Baseline is IC2 R2 `3d96581c2eef324df3b5ed69f7ba526bb37035fc`, including Financial register19.0.1.1.0. The user authorized extending All-mode cards to sales summaries and ordinary POS without counting sales twice. Cash mode and its existing report oracle remain unchanged.

## G0 — scope and accounting definitions

Keep the existing five-card area and customer/supplier sections. Customer totals become **Net sales**, covering posted customer invoices/credit notes/receipts and the native POS accounting contributions below. Supplier invoice indicators retain their existing definitions. The other cards retain Settled, Outstanding, Partially settled operations, and Net overdue. No new ledger, stored financial amount, posting, reconciliation, schema table, native Odoo edit, or historical data repair.

Settled means settlement of the recorded customer receivable, including existing native credits/write-offs. It does not mean cash received by a bank. Summary application payments transfer the customer receivable into a separate `asset_current` platform clearing account; the customer claim can therefore be fully settled while the platform has not remitted cash. Do not add platform clearing residuals to customer Outstanding. Cash mode continues answering actual cash movement.

Dates remain `account.move.date`, and residuals are current at query time, matching FL1. Posted signed reversal events retain their own accounting date. Do not filter original posted summary entries by current summary/order state: IC2 cancellation is evidenced by the posted reversal, and correction by original + reversal + replacement. Archived summaries still have the same accounting effect.

## G1 — bounds and acceptance

Retain native80-row pagination and set-based aggregation; do not materialize all move IDs in the browser or do per-row queries. SQL recursion handles reversal ancestry with deduplicating `UNION`, so cycles cannot loop. It returns only eligible posted ledger row identities, not copied source records. Measure the actual isolated fixture and bounded generated ledger sample; no100k/production claim without measurement. No new dependency, cache, queue, or SQL view.

Inherited design assumption:20 concurrent readers,100000 moves/400000 lines, warm2second target. These are capacity targets only; final evidence states actual tested sample/concurrency and observed timing.

Acceptance covers: VAT-inclusive summary with mixed cash/bank/platform; ordinary closed uninvoiced POS; invoiced-before-close POS without duplicate session sales; mixed invoiced/uninvoiced session; invoiced-after-close native reclassification; refunds and refund invoice; summary cancel and edit/replacement; reversal of a POS reversal; split/pay-later receipts including subsequent partial/full settlement; stock/COGS and cash rounding not inflating revenue; foreign company currency grouped separately; current maturity/overdue; current domain/date/currency facet and every card drill matching the same row contributions. Verify cashier denied and payroll/company rules preserved, malformed/private domain operators denied, no writes during reporting, and hard-rollback fixtures.

## G2 — one read-only ledger projection

### Eligible rows

1. Existing invoice branch: native `move_type` in out_invoice/out_refund/out_receipt or in_invoice/in_refund/in_receipt, state posted. Preserve current native invoice signed totals/residuals and supplier direction normalization. A POS invoice is already here; never add its POS order total separately.
2. POS roots: native primary `pos.session.move_id`, session state closed, move_type entry. This includes the dedicated summary session. `account.move.baseer_pos_summary_id` alone is NOT primary identity: it also tags receipt and reversal entries.
3. Native late-invoice reclassification roots: posted entry with `account.move.reversed_pos_order_id` linked to an order from a closed session. Native `_generate_pos_order_invoice` leaves the old primary session unchanged and adds this signed reclassification, so excluding it double-counts a late-created invoice.
4. Follow `account.move.reversed_entry_id` descendants of roots2/3, recursively and in the same company, restricted to journal entries. Include their signed posted contribution. A summary receipt is not a root, so its reversal is not included. A reversed customer invoice remains in the ordinary invoice branch, not this POS journal branch.

Construct ancestry independently of the user's date/partner/journal facets, then intersect contributing posted moves with the full current move query. This allows a reversal inside the selected period whose original is outside it. Use a duplicate-free root/descendant identity set; a move having more than one qualifying native link must still contribute once. No name/ref matching, no generic income-account journal inclusion, no `pos.order.amount_total` addition, and no receipt/payment journal addition.

Membership reads only fixed native relation keys and session state. All reported amounts and IDs remain intersected with the actual caller's `account.move._search` and `account.move.line._search` queries. Do not sudo source browsing, accounting, amounts, or search. Opening a source continues checking that source model's read access. No POS/HR/accounting group is granted to make the report work.

### Amounts and precision

For each eligible POS journal entry, use **all actual `asset_receivable` account lines**, regardless of `display_type`: native split-payment receivable lines can have display_type product. Aggregate company-currency `balance::numeric` and `amount_residual::numeric` through the caller's AML query, also explicitly intersected with the caller's move query.

`total = SUM(receivable balance)`; `outstanding = SUM(receivable amount_residual)`; `settled = total - outstanding`. Signed refunds/reversals remain negative. Native invoiced-order intermediary debits and invoice-payment offset credits cancel in the primary session, leaving only unbilled sales. Native actual receivable totals include tax and cash rounding; stock/COGS and treasury counterpart lines are outside this projection. Never use entry.amount_total_signed (debit turnover), entry.amount_residual_signed (native always0), current exchange rates, or re-run native tax calculation.

The company/default POS intermediary and customer accounts must have the native expected receivable classification. The UI domain enforces this for method selection, but the native company default has no equivalent server constraint. Unsupported/misclassified source evidence must be surfaced as incomplete coverage, not silently treated as zero sales or estimated using names. The fixture validates configured source accounts and the primary receivable gross against actual native posting evidence.

Overdue for POS entries is the signed sum of the same receivable residual lines having a real `date_maturity < today`; no invented maturity for lines with no due date. Unlike invoice term filtering, do not require display_type payment_term. Partially settled POS operations are counted once per contributing entry when rounded nonzero residual and rounded settled have the same direction as nonzero total (equivalently 0 < abs(residual) < abs(total), matching signs). Net-zero/mixed-sign sessions may have a residual but are not falsely assigned native invoice payment_state. Native invoice partial-count semantics remain unchanged. Label the combined count as operations, not invoice/order count.

Use Decimal at the Python boundary, numeric SQL casts before arithmetic, existing native currency rounding and Western-number formatting. Group by company currency exactly as FL1; never combine currencies. A tiny rounded-zero contribution cannot satisfy a nonzero card predicate.

### Shared rows, cards, and searches

Expose nonstored monetary/source/status projections on existing account.move, computed in batches from the same contribution SQL. All-mode contribution amounts use company currency, including native invoice signed values and normalized supplier direction, so signed row sums match cards. Native original document amount/currency may remain optional fields. Rows outside invoice/POS sales contributions remain visible in All-mode with their existing journal/source presentation, but do not enter sales/invoice cards. Surface incomplete POS-account classification as a read-only coverage indicator; never modify the source configuration.

New monetary/status projections and their search methods enforce the existing accounting register gate, including explicit cashier denial, before querying. Field access groups restrict the new accounting-only fields; the compute still checks the gate so accidental mixed groups do not expose them. Do not attach new privileged calculations to native POS loader fields or change existing invoice payment_state. No model ACL, record rule, preset or user membership changes.

The five card domains use searchable contribution predicates generated from this same projection. Total selects invoice/POS contributor families; Settled and Outstanding select rounded nonzero contributions; Partial selects the above predicate/native invoice status; Overdue selects nonzero eligible maturity evidence. Intersect every card domain with current search facets. Clicking a card must reveal exactly the contributing native move rows, including necessary negative reclassification/reversal rows; rows must show those signed amounts rather than native debit turnover. Existing customer filter must expand to customer invoice/POS sales contributors. Native invoice-only payment-status filters remain clearly invoice-specific unless replaced by the shared status predicates.

Do not call `pos.session._accumulate_amounts` from reports: it increments partner customer_rank and reads stock with sudo. Do not call any native invoice creation, close, payment, reconciliation or reversal API while projecting data.

### G1 — smallest bounded implementation and ownership

### G2 — provenance, amounts and non-duplication

Recognized origin: a closed native POS session with an explicit Applications payment method, and its native account.payment linked by `pos_session_id` plus `pos_payment_method_id`. The original posted receipt move supplies the actual company-currency clearing AML on that method's `outstanding_account_id`; require current-asset, reconcilable, same-company evidence and a posted primary sale. For approved summaries these semantic fields are immutable after approval, and the summary's own `_verify_posting` already proved gross allocations against native clearing balances. Never add declared allocation money without posted native evidence.

Attach Applications sale contribution to the **primary sale lineage**, not the receipt's current settlement state. The original posted clearing balance supplies the recognized gross, including VAT. Posted primary-sale reversal descendants negate/reinstate that original contribution using exact native reversal lineage; replacement summaries supply their own new origin. A standalone reversal of a receipt does not cancel a sale. Ordinary POS refunds use their native signed negative origin. A late-created POS invoice must not add the same Applications sale again; this adjustment is payment-method sales provenance, not a second invoice total. Any ambiguous or unsupported origin is surfaced as incomplete evidence rather than estimated.

Incoming for the selected period is: **original incoming fragments + signed Applications sale contributions in that period − original incoming fragments attributable to collection of those already recognized Applications origins − signed original outgoing-refund fragments attributable to already recognized negative Applications sale origins**. The last fragments are negative, so their adjustment adds back their magnitude to Incoming while Outgoing stays actual. This symmetric settlement adjustment avoids counting both a negative sale and its actual refund twice. Origin discovery may cross the period boundary; emitted sale and cash rows use their own posted dates. A sale in month1 is included in month1, while its partial or full platform bank remittance in month2 contributes zero duplicate incoming in month2. Likewise a refund sale recognized in month1 has no second net recognition when its cash refund is paid in month2. Multiple settlement rows must adjust only their own exact allocated amounts. Do not adjust an entire mixed bank receipt, a customer's unrelated payment, an unmatched opening balance, or a generic transfer merely because it uses the same account.

The minimal trace extension is read-only provenance decoration: while the original handler traverses a known platform receipt's non-clearing counterpart, mark the returned native fragments with that recognized signed sale origin. The original handler continues to allocate partial reconciliation, signed fee/discount counterparts, rounding and cash direction. Capture subtracts the proven collection/refund fragment from Incoming as described above; all original outgoing fragments remain unchanged. Negative-refund adjustment requires a proven negative sale or primary-sale reversal origin. A standalone receipt reversal or arbitrary platform cash payment is insufficient. Provenance is a private Python reporting state, not a caller-controlled context bypass. Where a path cannot prove its Applications origin, retain the original cash fragment and display an incomplete-attribution warning; do not silently suppress cash.

Fees and refunds require explicit parity: a net bank remittance of90 for an already recognized100 platform sale must not introduce a second100 incoming. Remove only the attributed100 collection fragment; keep the native signed fee fragment in its original direction (the existing report can classify a netted fee as negative incoming, rather than outgoing). Never invent an extra10 bank payment. A690 platform sale followed by a−690 recognized refund and an actual−690 bank refund has a+690 incoming settlement adjustment, producing lifecycle net0 rather than−690. The adjustment may appear in a later month than the negative sale; it prevents duplicate recognition and does not remove actual outgoing. A cancelled/replaced summary follows posted primary reversal lineage, including when the original lies outside the selected month. Existing cash transfer exclusions and payroll/accounting privacy remain intact.

Every displayed amount/source must pass the caller's account.move and account.move.line rules, and reconciliation-edge access where traversed. Source metadata identifies provenance without granting POS configuration, HR or additional accounting rights. No sudo monetary/source query, broad category account exclusion, supplied amount authority or source-name inference. Insufficient ACL evidence produces the existing controlled denial or honest incomplete coverage, not hidden-data totals.
