# NSM-PRODUCT-1 — Noorix product-master QA migration

Classification: **ARCHITECTURAL**.  Status: owner-approved QA execution; independent delivery review and any production decision remain separate.  Owner: migration lead.  Alternate: independent migration reviewer.  Reference contract: `docs/operations/2026-09-12-noorix-supplier-migration/PRODUCTS-COMPANY-SCOPED-PLAN.md`.

## Scope and boundaries

The SHA-pinned Noorix archive is the immutable source.  Only the isolated QA database `baseer_noorix_data_migration_qa_20260912` may receive this wave.  The protected original database `baseer_dev`, source archive, stock quantities, valuation, prices, taxes, accounts, journals, invoices, purchases and sales are outside scope.

The writer creates 467 active, company-scoped `product.template` records from 468 active source-item identities: `ARZ → company 1` (382 target products) and `المعلم الشامي → company 2` (85 target products).  The owner-approved ARZ avocado pair maps two immutable source rows to one deterministic target.  No product has `company_id=False`, and no existing QA product is reused.

## Target-master contract

- Create 47 global technical product-category leaves below existing `Food` or `Goods` roots.  Categories may be shared as technical classification; each product remains company-scoped.
- Reuse existing QA UoMs for `حبة`, `جرام`, `كيلو`, `لتر` and `مل`.  Create four separate root UoMs—`can/carton`, `packet`, `صندوق`, `قارورة`—with factor 1 and no conversion relationship to `الوحدات` or to one another.
- Preserve Arabic and English product labels through Odoo translations; missing product English labels are supplied except that no new Shisha-product translation is inferred.  Odoo 19's installed `product.category.name` is not translatable, so each new category stores its approved Arabic display label only; its approved English counterpart remains in the SHA-pinned payload and provenance evidence rather than being forced into an unsupported target field.
- The strict product allowlist is `name` in `ar_001` and `en_US`, `company_id`, `categ_id`, `uom_id`, `type='consu'`, `is_storable` from Noorix `track_inventory`, `purchase_ok`, `sale_ok`, `active=True` and `tracking='none'`.  Odoo 19 has no `uom_po_id` field in this target model, so the mapped inventory UoM is the only UoM field written.  No SKU is written because every Noorix SKU is empty.
- The writer must not write standard price, price lists, vendor lines, routes, taxes, accounting properties, valuation, stock quantities, descriptions, images or any product field outside this allowlist.  Setting `is_storable=True` records the source inventory-tracking intent only; it does not create any stock quantity or valuation.
- Category creation has an allowlist of the approved Arabic display `name` and `parent_id` only.  The post-check reads the new category's stored `property_*`, cost, valuation and account columns and asserts they are all NULL; inherited company defaults shown by Odoo on read are not treated as migration writes.  It also asserts that no existing root/category/UoM was written.
- Odoo 19 models UoM groups as a root `uom.uom` and descendants, not with the former `uom.category` model.  Each package label is therefore one standalone root UoM with `relative_uom_id=False`, `relative_factor=1`, `factor=1`, no package type, and no descendant or conversion relationship.  The post-check asserts that those four roots cannot be converted to `الوحدات` or to one another.

## Provenance, idempotency and recovery

The write-free deterministic payload is stored under the private QA evidence run directory and pinned by SHA-256 before the writer starts.  Its schema records, for every source item, the source identity, raw-row SHA-256, canonical product decision, target company, Arabic/English labels, category target or fallback, target UoM, storage flag and sale/purchase flags.  It separately records each active source-category identity and source-UoM identity with its target identity, decision and raw-row SHA-256.

The source partition is fixed: 59 product-category records = 56 active-referenced category identities + 2 inactive-only referenced identities (`FOOD`, `OFFER`) + 1 unreferenced identity (`بترول`); 56 active source-category identities resolve to 47 canonical category leaves, while 21 active items have no source category and use the explicit `Goods` fallback.  The 75 source-UoM records = 12 active-referenced identities + 63 not used by an active product; the 12 identities resolve to 9 target UoMs (five existing and four new package roots).

The QA-only migration add-on depends directly on official Odoo `product` (product template/category/UoM) and `stock` (the source-backed `is_storable` field); preflight asserts both are installed and compatible in QA and adds no external package.  Preflight also asserts that `ar_001` and `en_US` are active and that ORM translation write/read round-trips succeed for `product.template` and `uom.uom`; it verifies the single Arabic `product.category.name` round-trip separately because that Odoo 19 field has no translation support.  The add-on is extended with three append-only mapping models that do not alter the existing supplier mapping models: `baseer.noorix.product.map → product.template`, `baseer.noorix.product.category.map → product.category`, and `baseer.noorix.product.uom.map → uom.uom`.  Each stores `source_system`, `source_tenant_id`, `source_company_id`, its entity-specific source identifier, `source_row_sha256`, `source_archive_sha256`, `canonical_key`, `decision`, its exact target relation and `run_id`; each has a full unique source-identity constraint, writer-only create, and denied write/unlink.  The writer uses the Odoo ORM, a transaction-scoped PostgreSQL advisory lock derived from the database plus `noorix-product-master`, deterministic payload SHA-256 and preflight target checks.  One atomic transaction is used because the run is bounded to 467 product creations and must complete within 60 seconds; any failure rolls back the entire master-data/product/map wave.  A replay of the same committed payload verifies and exits; a changed source row, target identity or payload conflicts before writing.

The writer records the run in the existing private run model under the `product_master` scope.  It takes its advisory lock before looking up the run identity.  Reconciliation and every replay assert payload-exact source identity/hash/decision evidence, source-map count 468, distinct product count 467, company split 382/85, zero shared mapped products, 56 active source-category maps, 12 source-UoM maps, four non-convertible package UoMs, NULL stored accounting columns on the 47 created categories, and exact approved product relation/purchase/sale/storage/translation fields.  Every successful replay appends a timestamped verifier-artifact SHA-256 receipt to the private run result.  Before the write it records one coherent QA recovery manifest: custom-format database dump SHA-256, deterministic filestore content-manifest SHA-256, filestore archive SHA-256, add-on source revision/version and runtime image digest.  Recovery restores that exact database/filestore pair to a disposable clone and opens a stored attachment through Odoo; no original database is a rollback target.

## Direct path

This extends the installed QA-only add-on and reuses the Odoo 19 ORM, PostgreSQL and the existing local writer pattern.  It adds no package, external service, UI or financial authority.  The direct path is shorter and safer than Excel import because it provides immutable source lineage, repeat protection, company enforcement and machine-checkable reconciliation.

## Approved QA purchase-cost extension

This is a distinct, owner-approved QA-only wave, not a production price decision.  It consumes payload `f9894e12c31cd3e5d0408cf6880e7892800a4fc3496beaaff581bc6d07168d7c` from the same SHA-pinned Noorix archive and sets only the company-dependent Odoo field `product.product.standard_price` for 277 purchase-only targets (193 company 1 and 84 company 2).  The selection is the latest non-zero received-purchase inventory-unit cost per canonical product; the avocado canonical target uses the later approved source cost of 20.36.  It excludes 86 purchase-only targets without an eligible cost, the one zero-cost floor mop and all 103 sale-only products.

The QA-only add-on supplies an append-only `baseer.noorix.product.cost.map` mapping model.  Each record keeps its stable Noorix price-history identity, source-row hash, canonical product key, effective time, selected source cost, exact `product.product` target and run identity; the source identity is unique and writes/unlinks are denied outside the superuser migration-writer context.  The writer locks `noorix-product-cost` before run lookup, verifies the payload SHA and master-map baseline, requires every target to be active and purchase-only with a zero existing cost, writes all maps and costs atomically, flushes, and verifies every value and the accounting guard before commit.  A same-payload replay verifies all 277 mappings and target costs without writing.

Before the successful cost run, the process stored a coherent QA database-and-filestore recovery snapshot.  Accounting was treated as a hard guard: exact individual, batch and full-writer savepoint probes produced zero accounting moves; the committed run also preserved the QA `account.move` count at 2 and requires no `stock.valuation.layer` model.  The successful run is `20260912-noorix-product-cost-qa-1` (run ID 15).  It does not write `list_price`, sales price lists, vendor lines, taxes, currency, stock quantities, valuation layers or financial documents.  The 103 sale-only products therefore retain their pre-existing Odoo default `list_price=1.00`, which is not a Noorix selling-price import.  The protected `baseer_dev` stays out of scope; any promotion or sales-price migration needs a separately approved source, payload, QA review and delivery decision.

The reconciled replay verifies every stored cost-map provenance field against the SHA-pinned payload, including source-system/tenant/company/product/history identities, raw-row/archive hashes, canonical key, decision, effective time and exact company-scoped purchase-only target.  Its receipt in run 15 links both the executed-writer and verifier artifacts.  A disposable recovery smoke restored the pre-cost database/filestore pair, opened a stored attachment through Odoo, then removed the clone.  This proves recoverability of the QA wave; it is not a production-promotion decision.
